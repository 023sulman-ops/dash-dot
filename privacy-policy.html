Privacy Policy - Dash Dot

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who we are
Dash Dot (the game) is developed by Muhammad Suleman (Pakistan). Contact: sulemanikot@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device
The game does not need an account. Your progress (best score, coin balance, chosen pet and outfit, unlocked items and settings such as sound, music and vibration) is saved only on your device. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. Information we do not collect
We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

4. Advertising
The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure results and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Watching rewarded ads is always optional.

5. Virtual coins
Coins and unlockable items are virtual. They have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money or any other currency, including cryptocurrency.

6. Children
Dash Dot is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will help.

7. Sharing and security
We do not sell personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above. Game progress stays on your device.

8. Your choices and deletion
You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. For ad data, use your Google and device ad settings. You can also email us with any privacy request.

9. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
